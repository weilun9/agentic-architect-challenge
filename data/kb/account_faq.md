# SkyVault Account FAQ

## Changing the account email
Go to Settings > Account > Email and enter the new address. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and the change takes effect once the link is opened. The link expires after 24 hours.

## Resetting a password
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. If two-factor authentication is on, you will also need your authenticator code or a backup code.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication under Settings > Security. SkyVault supports authenticator apps and hardware security keys. Each account gets 10 single-use backup codes.

## Deleting an account
The account owner can delete the account under Settings > Account > Delete account. All files are permanently removed 30 days after deletion, and this cannot be undone after that period.

## Contacting support
Support answers email every day. Business plan customers also have phone support on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 Singapore time.
