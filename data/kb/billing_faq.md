# SkyVault Billing FAQ

## Which plans are available
SkyVault offers three plans. Personal costs USD 4 per month for 200 GB. Pro costs USD 10 per user per month for 2 TB per user. Business costs USD 18 per user per month for 5 TB per user and includes the uptime commitment. Paying annually gives a 20% discount on any plan.

## How to update a payment method
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and choose Replace card. The new card is used from the next invoice. Expired cards are retried 3 times over 7 days before the account moves to read-only mode.

## Where to find invoices
Invoices are emailed to the account owner and are also listed under Settings > Billing > Invoices, where each one can be downloaded as a PDF.

## Changing or cancelling a plan
Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated on the next invoice. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan.

## Why a payment failed
Payments usually fail because the card has expired, the bank blocked an overseas charge, or the billing address does not match. Updating the card or contacting the bank fixes most failures.
