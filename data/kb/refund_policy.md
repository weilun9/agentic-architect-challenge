# SkyVault Refund Policy

## Who can approve a refund
Refunds are approved only by the SkyVault Billing team after they review the account. Support agents can explain this policy and open a refund request, but they cannot promise, guarantee or approve a refund.

## Monthly plans
A new monthly subscription can be refunded in full if the refund is requested within 14 days of the first payment. Monthly renewal payments are not refundable. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing period.

## Annual plans
An annual subscription can be refunded in full if the refund is requested within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, and the plan stays active until the end of its term.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, or charged for a plan you did not select, the incorrect charge is refunded in full once the Billing team has verified it.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days after approval, depending on the bank.

## Service credits for downtime
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Business plan customers can request a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Service credits are applied to the next invoice; they are not paid out as cash refunds. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

## Non-refundable items
Add-on storage packs that have already been used, and SkyVault gift cards, are not refundable.
