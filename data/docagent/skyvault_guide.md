# SkyVault Plans and Usage Guide (sample document, fictional company)

## Plans and prices
SkyVault has three plans. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.
- Personal: USD 4 per month, 1 user, 200 GB of storage.
- Pro: USD 10 per user per month, 2 TB of storage per user, minimum 2 users.
- Business: USD 18 per user per month, 5 TB of storage per user, minimum 5 users, maximum 500 users.

Paying for a year in advance gives a 20% discount on the monthly price of any plan.

## Extra storage
Any plan can add storage packs. A 1 TB pack costs USD 6 per month. A 10 TB pack costs USD 50 per month. Packs are shared by everyone on the account and can be removed at the end of any billing month.

## File limits
The largest single file you can upload is 50 GB with the desktop app and 5 GB through the web app. Version history keeps earlier versions of a file for 30 days on Personal and Pro, and for 180 days on Business.

## Uptime commitment
Business plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment. If uptime falls below 99.9%, the customer can request a service credit of 10% of that month's fee; below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. A 30-day month has 43,200 minutes.

## Support
Email support is available every day. Business plans also include phone support on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 Singapore time, and a named account manager for teams of 50 users or more.

## Data location
Customers choose a storage region when the account is created: Singapore, Frankfurt or Virginia. Files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Changing region later requires a migration that the support team schedules.
