# Coral bleaching - Wikipedia

- Rising temperatures trigger mass bleaching, affecting ~84.4% of reefs in 2024 and causing severe mortality rates up to 93%.
- Stressors like heatwaves, acidification, and pollutants drive rapid coral cover loss, reversing five years of gains by 2026.
- Reefs support a quarter of marine species but face collapse risks from disease outbreaks and declining fish diversity.
- Recovery depends on juvenile density and herbivorous fish biomass, while economic services are valued between $130k–$1.2M per hectare annually.
- Mitigation programs offer high benefit-cost ratios, yet future decalcification remains projected under current CO2 scenarios.
